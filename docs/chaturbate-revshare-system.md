# Chaturbate RevShare-First Affiliate System

## Business goal

NightCamFinds is designed around Chaturbate RevShare as the primary monetization model.

The objective is not simply to maximize outbound clicks. The objective is to acquire users who become economically valuable Chaturbate members and generate recurring affiliate revenue.

## Chaturbate program

Current affiliate campaign slug:

`C6yxN`

The observed program terms are:

- 20% of referred broadcaster money spent
- $50 per referred broadcaster
- 5% of referred affiliate income

NightCamFinds should therefore treat the 20% RevShare stream as the primary KPI and use the broadcaster referral component as secondary revenue.

## Tracking principle

Every outbound Chaturbate click generated by NightCamFinds must carry a NightCamFinds click identifier where the destination supports it.

The system should preserve:

- click_id
- source
- medium
- campaign
- content
- landing_path
- placement
- country
- device
- room username
- Chaturbate tour/link code when applicable

## Conversion and revenue model

The revenue ledger must distinguish at least:

1. `revshare_money_spent`
2. `broadcaster_signup`
3. `referred_affiliate_income`
4. `other`

Do not collapse these into a single generic conversion type. This allows the dashboard to answer whether growth is coming from member spending, broadcaster referrals, or second-tier affiliate income.

## Dashboard KPIs

Primary:

- Chaturbate RevShare revenue
- RevShare revenue by day
- RevShare revenue by country
- RevShare revenue by source
- RevShare revenue by campaign
- RevShare revenue by landing page
- RevShare revenue by room/category
- RevShare EPC
- revenue per visitor

Secondary:

- broadcaster referral revenue
- referred affiliate income
- registrations
- engaged hits
- raw hits

## Important attribution limitation

The Chaturbate Stats page can provide aggregate RevShare statistics, while the dynamic postback can provide event-level data. NightCamFinds should not invent a click-to-revenue match when Chaturbate does not send a usable click identifier for that revenue event.

When a postback has a matching `click_id`, mark the revenue as attributed.

When it does not, store the event as `unmatched` and retain the raw payload. Aggregate Chaturbate RevShare totals can then be reconciled separately.

## Recommended traffic strategy

Optimize for qualified Tier-1 traffic rather than raw clicks. A source that produces fewer clicks but materially higher RevShare revenue should outrank a high-volume source with weak monetization.

Core reporting dimensions:

`source -> country -> landing page -> placement -> platform -> revenue`

## Target operating metric

The system should make it possible to calculate:

`RevShare EPC = attributed RevShare revenue / qualifying Chaturbate clicks`

and, where visitor data is available:

`RevShare revenue per visitor = RevShare revenue / visitors`

These metrics should drive decisions about where to spend organic effort or paid traffic budget.
